Zepto delivers orders within 10 minutes in serviceable areas using localized dark stores. Delivery operates from 6:00 AM to 2:00 AM daily. Standard delivery fee is INR 25 on orders below INR 149; orders of INR 149 and above qualify for free standard delivery. During peak hours or inclement weather, dynamic surge pricing of INR 15 to INR 30 may apply to ensure delivery partner availability.
